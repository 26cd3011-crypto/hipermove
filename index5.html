PRIVACY AND USE

Standalone demo privacy

This Opera-ready file processes the selected video, or your live camera feed if you turn it on, in your browser. Camera frames are never recorded or uploaded, and the camera switches off when you leave live mode or close the page. It does not upload the video or raw pose landmarks. The offline recap is calculated locally, and no session measurements are sent to a HiperMove server.

MediaPipe's library and pose model are loaded from Google and jsDelivr when you continue, so an internet connection is required. MediaPipe may send usage and performance metrics to Google. The consent screen explains this before the model loads. Read MediaPipe's terms and privacy notice.

This prototype is intended for adults 18 or older. The checkbox is a self-attestation, not technical age verification. Use only footage you have permission to process, including permission from anyone shown.

Movement values are estimates, not medical, safety, or exercise coaching advice.
