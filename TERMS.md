# HiperMove prototype terms of use

HiperMove is a hack-day software prototype for movement exploration. It is provided as-is and without warranty under the MIT license for its original code.

Use the camera only after granting the browser permission. Start it only for people who consent to being in frame. Do not use HiperMove to identify, surveil, evaluate, or make decisions about a person. Do not use the estimates for medical, clinical, rehabilitation, safety, or exercise-coaching decisions.

Pose and movement outputs are approximate and can be incomplete or wrong. The app is not a medical device or a substitute for a qualified professional.

The optional Gemma 4 feature is disabled unless the operator supplies a server-side Gemini API key and explicitly configures an audience aged 18 or older. Operators must accept and comply with Google's current API terms, respect applicable privacy and consent requirements, and keep secrets out of the public repository.

The operator of a public instance is responsible for its hosting, privacy contact, age restrictions, data notices, access controls, and compliance review. These prototype notes are not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction.
